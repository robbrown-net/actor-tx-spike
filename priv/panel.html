Back-pressure: admission limit

The most transactions the system will run at once. Beyond it a submission is refused with "overloaded" before anything starts, and the sender waits and tries again (counted as throttled).

Send with the header settings

Every transaction uses the merchant, customer, region, type and currencies currently chosen in the header; only the amount varies.

Randomise everything

Each transaction draws its own settings at random, so you meet refusals (forward to IN, retail to CN) and every corridor. Untick a setting to keep it as in the header.
